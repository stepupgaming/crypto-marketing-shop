How to calculate KOL cost per wallet

For people who already pay creators. This is campaign math, not a token call.

Cost per wallet (CPW) is what you paid a creator, divided by how many unique wallets actually connected or signed after that post. That is it.

CPW = creator fee (plus agreed extras) ÷ unique wallet connects attributed to that placement

What counts as a wallet

Pick one definition before the campaign starts and do not change it mid-flight:

  • Wallet connect on your site or dapp (the cleanest default)
  • Signed message / allowlist join, if connect is too cheap to be useful
  • First on-chain action you can actually see (swap, mint, stake). Only use this if you can attribute it without guessing

Clicks are not wallets. Likes are not wallets. “Impressions to market cap” is not a metric you can defend.

What to put in the fee

Use cash you actually paid: flat fee, usage rights, boost budget you handed the creator, and any agency cut on that placement. Do not stuff in your own salary or the token’s price move.

A worked example (made-up numbers)

You pay a creator $2,000. Your tracker shows 80 unique wallet connects on the UTM from that post, and you are not double-counting people who also came from your own account.

2000 ÷ 80 = $25 CPW

Example only. Your sheet will be different. Nothing here is a target or a promise.

How to attribute without lying to yourself

  1. Give each creator a unique UTM or link.
  2. Log the post URL, go-live time, and fee on the same row.
  3. Count unique wallets in a fixed window (24h, 72h, or 7 days). Write the window down.
  4. If two creators share an audience, do not add both rows as if they were strangers.

Disclosures

If a creator is paid, they need to say so. That is not optional in the US. Keep a checkbox per post. We are not your lawyer.
