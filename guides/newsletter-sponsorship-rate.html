How to price a web3 newsletter sponsorship

For people who already send a list. This is rate math, not a promise that a sponsor will convert.

A defensible starting rate is unique opens, times a CPM you can explain, divided by 1000. Subscriber count alone is a vanity number.

Base rate = (unique opens × CPM) ÷ 1000

Use unique opens, not the list size

Take the last 4 to 8 issues. Average unique opens, not sends. If you only have subscriber count, you do not have a rate yet. You have a hope.

Pick a CPM you can say out loud

A common starting band for a niche crypto or fintech letter is $20 to $60 CPM on unique opens. Higher only if you have category-proof (repeat sponsors, measured clicks, a tight audience). Lower if you are new or the list is mixed.

Write the CPM on the invoice so a sponsor can audit the math.

A worked example (made-up numbers)

Last six issues averaged 6,400 unique opens. You use a $32.55 CPM because that is what you actually charged last quarter.

(6400 × 32.55) ÷ 1000 = $208.32

Example only. Your list will be different. This is not a market rate we are selling as fact.

What to add on top

  • Primary slot vs secondary mention
  • A dedicated send vs a section in the regular issue
  • A second placement in the archive or social recap, only if you will actually do it

Do not multiply by a token price. Do not guarantee signups or wallet connects.

What the sponsor will ask

  1. Unique opens, not subscribers
  2. A recent issue they can read
  3. Whether you accept competitors in the same month
  4. Whether the placement is labeled as sponsored

If you cannot answer those, you are not ready to send a rate card.
